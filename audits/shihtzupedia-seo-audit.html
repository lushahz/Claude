Full SEO audit

shihtzupedia.com

Audited 28 September 2026 74 indexable URLs crawled (69 posts, 5 pages) 560 outbound links checked 3 Lighthouse mobile runs

The site's technical base and on-page setup are solid, and the September rewrite gave every post the same clean structure: a quick answer, a table of contents, FAQs and a list of sources. The biggest things holding it back are slow mobile loading (the main image is lazy-loaded), category hubs hidden from Google, weak internal linking to about a third of the posts, and missing pages for high-volume topics.

Top 5 priorities

  1. Stop lazy-loading the featured image and add a page cache

    On post pages the largest element (LCP) is the featured image, and Smush lazy-loads it (data-src + lazyload). The browser only fetches it after JavaScript runs, which pushes mobile LCP to 6.8–8.2 s. The server is LiteSpeed, but no page cache is active (no x-litespeed-cache header), so every page is built fresh and takes 530–700 ms before the first byte arrives.

    Impact: high · Effort: 1–2 hours · Tools: Smush exclusions, LiteSpeed Cache plugin

  2. Turn the 6 category pages into indexable hub pages

    /health/, /food/, /care/, /training/, /breed/ and /gear/ are linked from every page and from breadcrumbs, but they are noindex and missing from the sitemap. They should be the site's strongest topical pages.

    Impact: high · Effort: 1 day (write 150–300 word intros + curated link lists, then index)

  3. Fix internal linking to under-linked posts

    26 of 69 posts get two or fewer links from other articles. /shih-tzu-names-for-females/ gets none, and the FAQ page, which has 42 answers, gets one.

    Impact: high · Effort: 1 day

  4. Build pages for high-volume topics that are missing

    /best-dog-food-for-shih-tzu/ 301-redirects to the allergies article, so the head term lands on a narrower page. There are no dedicated pages for haircuts, male names, harnesses, tear stains, luxating patella or breed comparisons.

    Impact: high · Effort: ongoing, about 2 posts a week

  5. Add real experience and expert review (E-E-A-T)

    Pet health is close to YMYL. The About page says the editor is not a vet, and no posts are vet-reviewed. Adding a credentialed reviewer, original photos and first-hand owner notes would set the site apart from other sites that just summarise sources.

    Impact: medium–high · Effort: ongoing

CriticalThe LCP image is lazy-loaded on every post

The featured image img.post-top-featured has data-src and class="lazyload". Lighthouse flags "Preload LCP image" as failing.

Fix: In Smush → Lazy Load, exclude the class post-top-featured (and custom-logo). Make sure the image gets fetchpriority="high". Kadence adds this when the image isn't lazy-loaded.

CriticalNo full-page cache on a LiteSpeed server

Responses have no cache-control or x-litespeed-cache header. TTFB was 530–700 ms in tests.

Fix: Install LiteSpeed Cache (free, and Hostinger supports it). Turn on page cache, CSS/JS minify, and "Load CSS Asynchronously" (critical CSS). Turn on the guest mode optimisations. Leave Smush in charge of images, or switch images to LSCache and remove Smush to avoid running both.

HighRender-blocking CSS and the consent script

7–8 Kadence stylesheets plus cdn-cookieyes.com/…/script.js block the first paint. Lighthouse estimates 1.4–2.1 s of savings. Posts also load jQuery and jquery-migrate in the head.

Fix: Generate critical CSS and defer the rest (LSCache CCSS). Delay non-essential JS. Load CookieYes asynchronously if your consent setup allows it. Stop loading the Contact Form 7 CSS/JS except on /contact/.

HighAds and third-party JavaScript slow the homepage

AdSense blocks the main thread for about 300 ms and CookieYes for about 160 ms. Total TBT is 730 ms. Unused JS is 148 KiB, almost all from AdSense.

Fix: Remove Auto Ads from the homepage or lazy-load ad slots below the first screen. Keep every ad out of the hero.

MediumStatic assets are cached for only 7 days

Images, fonts, CSS and JS are served with max-age=604800.

Fix: Cache versioned assets and uploads for 1 year (max-age=31536000, immutable) with LSCache's browser cache or .htaccess.

GoodLayout stability and image formats

CLS is 0 (every image has width and height). WebP is served through content negotiation. Page weight is 766–846 KiB.

2. Crawl, indexing & technical

HighCategory hubs are noindexed

/health/ (27 posts), /breed/ (12), /training/ (12), /food/ (10), /gear/ (9) and /care/ (8) all return noindex, follow. They're linked site-wide in the menu and breadcrumbs. There's no category sitemap.

Fix: Add a unique intro, a "start here" list and short FAQs to each category. Set categories to index in Rank Math → Titles & Meta → Categories, and enable the category sitemap.

MediumTwo-hop redirect from http://www

http://www.shihtzupedia.com → https://www.shihtzupedia.com/ → https://shihtzupedia.com/

Fix: Add one .htaccess rule that sends every http and www variant to https://shihtzupedia.com in one hop.

MediumA head term redirects to a narrower page

/best-dog-food-for-shih-tzu/ 301s to /best-dog-food-for-shih-tzu-with-allergies/. Searchers who want general food picks land on an allergy-focused article.

Fix: Publish a real "Best Dog Food for Shih Tzus" roundup at that URL. Remove the redirect and link it to the allergy, sensitive stomach and puppy food pages.

Low/page/2/ returns 200 with index

It shows a duplicate of the static homepage and canonicalises to /, so there's low risk, but it's crawl waste.

Fix: Redirect /page/N/ to /, or set paginated archives to noindex in Rank Math.

LowNo ads.txt while AdSense is running

/ads.txt returns 404. The AdSense publisher is ca-pub-7314152360547879.

Fix: This is a revenue issue rather than a ranking one. Add google.com, pub-7314152360547879, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0.

GoodWhat's already right

HTTPS everywhere. robots.txt only blocks /wp-admin/. Sitemap index is listed in robots.txt. All 74 sitemap URLs return 200 with self-referencing canonicals. Search, tag, 404 and legal pages are correctly noindexed. Merged legacy URLs 301 to the right new pages (for example /the-miniature-shih-tzu/ → Imperial guide, /white-shih-tzus-a-must-read-guide/ → Colors). There are no broken internal links. All 223 Amazon links use rel="sponsored nofollow".

3. On-page SEO

Medium22 meta descriptions over 160 characters and 20 titles over 60

Longest titles: best dog food with allergies (70 chars), food calculator (68), and the directory, treats, chew toys, collar and human food pages (67). These get cut off on mobile.

Fix: Keep titles to about 55–60 characters with the main keyword first. Drop the "| Shih Tzu Pedia" suffix on long titles. Keep descriptions at 140–155 characters.

LowLong or misspelled slugs

/shih-tzu-temperatment-with-cats/ is misspelled. Ten slugs are over 7 words, for example /shih-tzu-characteristics-physical-traits-temperament-health-and-care-guide/ and /male-vs-female-shih-tzu-real-differences-in-size-temperament-health-and-care/.

Fix: Only change the misspelled slug (to /shih-tzu-and-cats/) with a 301. Leave the other long slugs alone because they already have history. Keep new slugs to 2–5 words.

Low72 of 218 image files have hash names

For example 71f1f25f40.jpg and 727645e045.jpg. The alt text is good, but file names help with Google Images.

Fix: Give new uploads descriptive names like shih-tzu-puppy-litter.jpg. Only rename old files with a redirect-aware plugin.

LowColour contrast fails

Lighthouse flags the hero eyebrow, the CTA buttons, the palette-1 coloured paragraphs, the tag links and the CookieYes "Accept" button. Accessibility score is 96–97.

Fix: Darken the palette-1 text colour so it reaches 4.5:1.

GoodHeadings, alt text and meta tags

Every page has exactly one H1 and a logical H2/H3 structure. No images are missing alt text. OG and Twitter cards are complete. Lighthouse SEO score is 100 on every page tested.

5. Content quality & E-E-A-T

HighNo expert reviewer on health content

27 health posts cover seizures, kidney failure, cancer, end of life and similar topics. The About page says plainly that "Shahzad is not a veterinarian". There's no "Reviewed by DVM" line and no reviewer in the schema.

Fix: Hire a freelance vet or vet tech to review the health posts (services like Vetted or Upwork work for this). Add "Medically reviewed by …" with a bio page, and add reviewedBy to the article schema. Start with the highest-risk pages.

HighEvery post follows the same template, with little first-hand experience

All 69 posts have the same structure: "How we researched this guide", "Quick answer", 3 images, TOC, FAQ, Sources, and "Last reviewed: September 2026". All 74 URLs show dateModified 2026-09-25. The content is accurate and sourced, but it reads like a summary of sources. Many images are stock photos with hash file names.

Fix: Add things only you can provide: your own Shih Tzu photos, owner case notes, groomer or breeder quotes, and original data (for example, a survey of your readers). Change the structure between post types, so buying guides get comparison tables and hands-on notes. Only update dateModified when the content really changes.

MediumAuthor profiles could carry more weight

The Person schema sameAs only lists shihtzupedia.com. Khizra K. (10 posts) has only a short archive description.

Fix: Add LinkedIn and social profiles to sameAs. Expand both author pages with experience, the dogs you own, and credentials.

Medium15 posts under 1,000 words

For example the guard dog (860 words), urinary problems (879) and summer haircuts (920) posts. Short is fine when the question is narrow. The haircuts post targets a broad, high-volume topic, though, and competitors show 8–13 styles with photos.

Fix: Expand /top-5-summer-haircuts-for-shih-tzus/ into an all-season "Shih Tzu Haircuts" guide covering the puppy, teddy bear, lion and top knot cuts, with clipper blade lengths and original photos.

GoodStrong trust basics

The site has About, Editorial Policy, Contact, Affiliate Disclosure and Medical Disclaimer pages. Articles cite AKC, AAHA, VCA, Merck, AVMA, PubMed and RVC VetCompass. Emergency advice sends readers to a vet.

6. Structured data

MediumFAQPage schema on all 69 posts

Since 2023 Google only shows FAQ rich results for well-known government and health sites. The markup is valid and does no harm, but it won't produce rich snippets here.

Fix: Keep the visible FAQs because they help with long-tail and AI answers. Don't expect SERP enhancements. Focus effort on reviewedBy, Product/ItemList on the buying guides, and HowTo-style steps in the article content.

LowSmall schema errors

On the homepage, primaryImageOfPage declares 200×200 but the file is 640×426. The Organization sameAs leaves out the Instagram profile linked in the header. The BlogPosting on the About, Contact and Editorial pages would fit better as AboutPage, ContactPage and WebPage.

Fix: Fix these in Rank Math's Schema tab for each page and under Titles & Meta → Local SEO / Social.

GoodComplete graph

Organization with a logo, WebSite with SearchAction, BreadcrumbList, WebPage, Person and BlogPosting with keywords and dates.

Method & limits

What I did: I crawled every URL in sitemap_index.xml as Googlebot and parsed titles, meta tags, headings, canonicals, robots, JSON-LD, images and links. I checked archives, 404s, redirects and legacy URLs, and requested all 560 outbound links. I ran Lighthouse 12 (mobile, simulated throttling) on 3 page templates.

Limits: Lighthouse ran from a cloud container through a proxy. Treat the numbers as directional, and ignore its "Use HTTP/2" warning, which comes from the proxy. The site serves HTTP/2 and HTTP/3. I couldn't get real-user (CrUX) data because the PageSpeed API quota was exhausted. I had no access to Search Console, GA4 or backlink tools, so this audit doesn't include rankings, traffic, index coverage or the backlink profile. Those would be the next layer.
